# 🎯 Weekly Practice System

Instead of randomly solving problems, use a weekly system.

### Monday — SQL

**5–10 SQL problems**

Focus on one topic.

### Tuesday — Python

**2–5 pandas/Python exercises**

Write the solution yourself before checking the answer.

### Wednesday — Visualization

Recreate one professional dashboard or chart.

### Thursday — Business Analysis

Take a dataset and answer:

> **"What decision should a business make from this data?"**

### Friday — Project Work

Spend time building your portfolio project.

### Saturday — Interview Practice

Timed SQL + business case.

### Sunday — Review

Document:

- What you learned
- What you struggled with
- What you built
- What you need to practise next

# 🎤 Interview Practice

When you're comfortable with the fundamentals, move from **untimed practice → timed practice**.

### Stage 1 — Learn

Understand the concept.

### Stage 2 — Solve

Solve without looking at the answer.

### Stage 3 — Time

Add interview-style time pressure.

### Stage 4 — Explain

Talk through your solution while solving.

### Stage 5 — Defend

Be ready for:

> "Why did you use this approach?"

> "What happens if the data contains NULLs?"

> "Can you make this query more efficient?"

> "What business decision would this analysis support?"

---

# 💡 The Most Important Practice Rule

> **Don't measure practice by how many tutorials you completed. Measure it by how many problems you can solve without help.**

And don't stop at:

**"I know SQL."**

Aim for:

**"Give me a business question and a dataset. I'll figure out how to answer it."**
