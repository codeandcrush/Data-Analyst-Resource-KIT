### 🛠️ Search Before You Ask

For technical problems:

```text
Error
 ↓
Google / Community Search
 ↓
Existing Solution
 ↓
Try It
 ↓
Ask With Context If Needed
```

When asking a technical question, include:

- What you're trying to achieve
- Sample data
- Your query/code
- Expected result
- Actual result
- Error message

This dramatically improves the quality of answers you receive.

# 📢 Share Your Work

Communities become much more valuable when you contribute.

Share:

- 📊 Dashboard screenshots
- 🧠 SQL solutions
- 🐍 Python notebooks
- 📈 Data visualizations
- 💡 Interesting findings
- 📝 Project writeups
- 🎯 Lessons learned
- 🔧 Useful resources

Instead of:

> "Here is my dashboard."

Try:

> **"I analysed 50K sales records and found that 18% of revenue came from a customer segment with significantly lower retention. Here's how I identified it and what I would recommend."**

That's a conversation starter.

# ⭐ Community Rules

### 1. Don't Spam

Don't post the same portfolio link everywhere.

### 2. Give Before Asking

Answer questions and share useful resources.

### 3. Be Specific

Good questions get better answers.

### 4. Show Your Work

A screenshot, query, notebook or example is better than:

> "My SQL isn't working."

### 5. Respect Different Opinions

Analytics often involves judgement and trade-offs.

### 6. Protect Data

Never share confidential company information, credentials or sensitive customer data.
