# 🎓 Free Learning Resources

> **Curated resources to support every phase of the Data Analyst roadmap.**
>
> Use these resources **when the roadmap tells you to** — don't try to complete everything.

---

## 🧭 How to Use This Section

```text
🗺️ Roadmap
    ↓
📚 Choose the required skill
    ↓
🎓 Pick 1 primary resource
    ↓
🧪 Practice
    ↓
🚀 Build a project
    ↓
🏆 Prove the milestone
```

### ⚠️ Don't resource-hop

You don't need to complete 10 courses for one skill.

**Pick one → finish the relevant material → practice → build.**
