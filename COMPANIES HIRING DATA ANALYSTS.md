# 🎯 How to Apply Strategically

Don't apply to 100 random jobs.

Build a target list.

### Step 1 — Choose Your Domain

```text
E-commerce
FinTech
SaaS
Consulting
Banking
Healthcare
Logistics
Product Analytics
```

### Step 2 — Choose Your Role

Search for:

```text
Data Analyst
Business Analyst
Product Analyst
Reporting Analyst
BI Analyst
Operations Analyst
Marketing Analyst
Data Analytics Associate
MIS Analyst
Junior Data Analyst
```

### Step 3 — Match Your Skills

```text
Excel
   +
SQL
   +
Power BI / Tableau
   +
Python
   +
Business Analysis
```

### Step 4 — Apply Directly

Use the company's official careers page.

### Step 5 — Track Everything

Maintain a simple application tracker:

| Company | Role | Date Applied | Resume Version | Status | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Company A | Data Analyst | 04-Oct | DA-v2 | Applied | 11-Oct |
| Company B | BI Analyst | 05-Oct | DA-v2 | Assessment | — |
| Company C | Business Analyst | 06-Oct | BA-v1 | Applied | 13-Oct |
